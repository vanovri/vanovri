Two original procedural Blender studies: a five-tier pagoda and an open pavilion. Includes generated GLBs, six actual Cycles renders, parameter files, evaluated-mesh evidence and an interactive Three.js viewer.

Independent visual review rejected the first build for roof penetration and clipped framing. These defects were corrected. The final review is bound to the actual GLB and render hashes. The original rejection remains in the repository.

Mechanical checks read exported GLB triangle positions directly, check closed oriented component topology, and sample roof contact around columns and along lintels. Regression tests reject an opened roof, raised lintel, detached column and altered GLB. Windows/Linux CI checks the published evidence; a separate Linux job regenerates the pavilion in Blender.

Nominal metre units. Individual closed components intentionally overlap. This is a stylized architectural study, with no claim of historical reconstruction, structural safety, a single watertight printable assembly, or an autonomous vision-model API. Subjective reviewer scores are opinions, not physical validation.
