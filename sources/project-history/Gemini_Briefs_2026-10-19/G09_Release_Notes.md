# Sensor Enclosure CAD v0.1.0

A hand-transcribed napkin sketch turned into deterministic **OpenSCAD** sources, real **OpenSCAD 2021.01** STL renders, and a geometric referee with a standalone 3D inspector.

**Design artifact only.** No photograph or OCR is included, no physical print was made, and nothing here guarantees fit, strength, ingress protection or manufacturability.

## What is verified

- 47 tests: spec validation, provenance and schema rejection, every gate failure mode, deterministic SCAD output, per-preset lid differences, STL parsing (binary and ASCII), manifoldness, orientation, volume, bounding box, ray-based wall thickness, port penetration, boss-hole depth, lid screw holes, and the committed mesh report.
- 20 / 20 spec gates and 17 / 17 mesh checks pass.
- Base, both lids and the assembly are watertight: zero boundary edges, zero non-manifold edges, zero degenerate triangles, consistent winding.
- Measured wall thickness is 2.40 mm on all four sides; the USB-C opening penetrates the front wall; the boss hole is blind at 10 mm; the boss ring has material.
- Volumes: base 42,039.8 mm³, lid 24,836.4 mm³, assembly 66,876.2 mm³ — the assembly equals the sum of its parts.
- Committed SCAD is regenerated byte-for-byte by `tools/verify.py`.
- Browser QA in Chromium (SwiftShader WebGL): **zero console errors**; lid preset, explode, section and wireframe all exercised. Evidence: `docs/browser-qa.json` plus four screenshots.
- Release ZIP is deterministic, entry-by-entry verified, and its remote SHA-256 matches.

## Limits

- Dimensions are a manual transcription of an example sketch; no real board was measured.
- No STEP export, FEA, tolerance stack-up, thermal, EMI or drop analysis.
- No printed prototype, screw test, snap-fit feature or IP rating.
- Section clipping is a viewer aid, not a section drawing.
- OpenSCAD renders depend on the 2021.01 series; facet order may differ across versions even when geometry matches.
